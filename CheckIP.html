Info
  • IP address, country, and internet provider: looked up using your public IP by an external geolocation service. The page tries ipwho.is first and ipapi.co if that request fails. Country and provider are estimates from provider databases and may be outdated or inaccurate; a VPN, proxy, mobile network, or corporate network can change what they report. The lookup services receive your IP address.
  • Operating system: inferred from information exposed by your browser. Windows 10 and Windows 11 commonly share the same standard browser identifier, so they can only be distinguished when the browser provides its additional platform version. Otherwise the page may show “Windows 10 / 11” or a less specific result. Other OS versions may also be hidden or reduced by the browser.
  • Browser: its name and version are parsed from the browser identifier. Browsers can hide or modify this information, and unrecognized browsers are shown as “Unknown.” The displayed version may not always be exact.
  • Time zone and local time: read from your device/browser settings. The time is displayed in 24-hour format and updates every second; incorrect device settings can make both values inaccurate.
  • Browser language: the preferred language reported by the browser, which may differ from your location or the language you are currently using on a website.
  • Device type: a best-effort guess based on the browser identifier. Tablets and devices with unusual or privacy-reduced identifiers may be classified incorrectly.
  • Screen resolution: the screen dimensions reported by the browser in CSS pixels, not necessarily the physical pixel count. Display scaling and browser/device settings can affect the reported values.
  • Network connection: the connection type or estimated quality, if the browser exposes this optional feature. Many browsers do not, so “Unavailable in this browser” does not mean the internet is disconnected.
